What's real, what isn't

Web push is real. If you allowed notifications on the customer page, the OS notification you get is genuinely delivered by the browser's push service — the tab does not need to be open.

SMS and WhatsApp are simulated. Sending either for real needs a licensed aggregator, a DLT-registered sender ID and approved templates. Rather than fake a delivery, the exact message is built, priced by segment, and shown here labelled simulated.
